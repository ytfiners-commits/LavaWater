🛡 Server Rules

Follow these rules to help keep LavaWater fair and enjoyable.

🤝

1. Respect Everyone

Be friendly to players and staff

RULE 01

No bullying, harassment, hate speech, threats, or excessive chat spam. Treat everyone with respect.

KEEP IT FRIENDLY Respect all players
🛡️

2. No Cheating

Play fair and earn your progress

RULE 02

No hacked clients, unfair cheats, exploiting bugs, or item duplication to gain an unfair advantage.

PLAY FAIR Earn your rewards
🏡

3. No Griefing

Protect builds and respect claims

RULE 03

Don't destroy other players' builds, steal their items, or interfere with their claims. Leave other people's property alone.

RESPECT BUILDS Leave claims alone
📜

4. Listen to Staff

Follow instructions and report problems

RULE 04

Don't impersonate staff or evade punishments. If you see a problem, report it calmly instead of starting an argument.

NEED HELP? Contact the staff team
